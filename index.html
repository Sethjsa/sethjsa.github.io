about

I'm Seth Aycock, a PhD candidate in multilingual NLP at the University of Amsterdam. I work with Christof Monz and Khalil Sima'an. My research interests can be summarised by these keywords:
machine translation, multilinguality, low-resource, linguistics, typology, reasoning, data.

I studied Linguistics (BA, Cambridge), and Speech & Language Processing (MSc, Edinburgh). I've worked in multilingual NLP research at Booking.com, Inria Paris, City University London, and the UK Governmen, with research visits to the University of Helsinki in Finland, and NII in Japan.
